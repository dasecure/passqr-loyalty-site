Why paper stops working

A punch card only counts when it's in the room.

Card stock, a stamp, a drawer full of half-finished cards. It's cheap to start and it quietly costs you every week.

It's at home, in a coat

The visit happens, the stamp doesn't. Every forgotten card is a regular you never got to count.

You can't reach anyone

A quiet Tuesday and no way to tell the people who already like you. Paper has no send button.

You're guessing

How many cards are live, who's close to a reward, which outlet actually stamps. Nobody knows.

How it works

Three moving parts. That's the whole system.

No POS integration, no hardware to buy, nothing to install on anyone's phone.

1

Put a QR on the counter

You pick your colours, your logo, how many stamps and what the reward is. We generate one printed QR per shop. It takes minutes, and nothing about it is per-customer.

2

They scan and tap once

The camera opens a page in your colours. One tap and the card is in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. No account, no form, no email — the pass itself is the identity.

3

Staff scan to stamp

Your counter phone or tablet opens the scanner in a browser and signs in once. Point it at the customer's card, and the stamp lands. Redeem is the same motion.

Anonymous by design

No account, no email, no app. The pass is the identity. The honest trade-off: lose the phone, lose the stamps — and we say so on the join page rather than hiding it.

The scanner is just a browser

Any phone or tablet you already own. Sign in once per counter and leave it on the till. No app store, no device management, no extra hardware bill.

Optional add-ons surface the card automatically when a customer arrives — a geofence around the shop, or a small beacon on the counter. Off by default; nothing to configure until you want it.

Questions

The things owners actually ask.

Do my customers have to download an app?

No. The card goes into Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, which are already on the phone. They scan the QR on your counter, tap once, and it's saved. There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for.

Does it work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. The join page detects the phone and hands an Apple Wallet pass to iPhone and a Google Wallet pass to Android. Your staff don't need to know which is which.

What do we scan with at the counter?

Any phone or tablet with a camera and a browser. The scanner runs at scan.passqr.com — you sign in once per counter and leave it open. There is no app store step and no hardware to buy.

What happens when the wifi drops?

The scanner keeps taking stamps and queues them on the device. When signal returns they replay, dated at the moment they were actually taken rather than the moment they uploaded — so a customer who bought a coffee at 9am doesn't get a 9:40am stamp. Repeated scans of the same event are ignored rather than double-counted.

Do we have to collect email addresses?

No, and by default you don't. The pass itself is the customer's identity, which is what makes enrolment a single tap. The honest cost: if a customer loses their phone, their stamps go with it, and you can't email people who only ever gave you a scan. If you'd rather build a mailing list, there's an optional claim link on the back of the card.

Can we send a message to everyone holding a card?

Yes. A broadcast writes to every live card and surfaces as a notification. Your plan includes a number of sends a month, free at any size. Past that, a send is $5 per 1,000 cards it reaches, and the console shows the price before you press send.

We have more than one location. Does that work?

Yes. Each shop gets its own counter login and its own printed QR, and every stamp is recorded against the outlet it happened at. Stamps earned at one site count on the same card at another, unless you'd rather they didn't.

How long does setup take?

Minutes for the card itself — your colours, your logo, the number of stamps and the reward. The demo does exactly that setup live, and you can keep what it builds you.

Does it plug into my POS?

It doesn't need to. Stamping is a separate scan at the counter, which is why it works for a market stall with no till as readily as for a café with one. If you want it wired to your point of sale, there's an API — tell us what you're running.
